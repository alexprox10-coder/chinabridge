# ChinaBridge — Business Model

## What We Do
B2B intermediary for importing goods from China to Russia (Blagoveshchensk, Heihe route) and Kazakhstan (Almaty, Astana, Shymkent). We handle sourcing, verification, purchasing, consolidation, and delivery.

## Revenue Streams
1. **Sourcing fee** — 15,000 ₽ per supplier search (3–5 verified factories)
2. **Factory audit** — 25,000 ₽ (on-site visit, video report, certificates)
3. **Purchasing commission** — 5–8% of order value (FX conversion + procurement control)
4. **Consolidation/cargo** — margin on per-kg rate (we buy at $2.5–4, charge $4–6+)
5. **Quality control** — 12,000 ₽ per inspection
6. **Turnkey service** — bundled pricing (highest margin, target product)
7. **SaaS** — AI tools for importers (Product Analyzer, Rate Calculator) — target MRR

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
**Primary (RU):**
- Marketplace sellers (WB/Ozon/Yandex) buying from China, order value 200k–5M ₽/month
- Seeking verified supplier + logistics + no FX hassle
- Pain: don't know suppliers, fear getting scammed, no China presence

**Primary (KZ):**
- Kaspi sellers and wholesale importers, 500k–10M ₽ equivalent
- Almaty-based businesses growing online presence
- Pain: grey scheme complexity, unreliable cargo, no agent in China

**Secondary:**
- Construction/industrial equipment buyers (large single orders)
- Retail chains expanding private label

## Unit Economics
- Average deal value (RU): ~150,000–500,000 ₽
- Average deal value (KZ): ~300,000–1,000,000 ₽
- GP margin on cargo: 30–50%
- GP margin on sourcing: ~80%
- Target LTV: 3+ repeat imports per year
